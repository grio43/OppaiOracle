## Truth inference / label aggregation from multiple noisy sources, with per-source AND per-direction (asymmetric ordinal) reliability calibrated from a small gold set, to produce a calibrated per-image posterior over the true hair-length ordinal label that drives abstain/auto/human-review routing

RECOMMENDED MODEL (concrete): start from a Bayesian Dawid-Skene / IBCC backbone (Kim & Ghahramani; Dawid-Skene) because it gives per-source full confusion matrices = per-direction reliability, then add two things the project specifically needs: (a) an ORDINAL-ADJACENCY prior on each confusion matrix (concentrate Dirichlet mass on the diagonal + adjacent off-diagonals, per Zhou et al. ordinal minimax-entropy) so it exploits that errors are adjacent; (b) a DEPENDENCY block tying the 3 Danbooru taggers (DBCC variant / Snorkel-MeTaL dependency graph) so their shared convention bias is discounted, not triple-counted. Use spectral init (Zhang et al.) to avoid anchoring EM on the biased majority vote.

ENCODING DIRECTION-AWARE ASYMMETRIC TRUST (the core requirement, three compatible mechanisms): (1) Because each source has a FULL asymmetric confusion matrix, the entries π[long→very_long] (trusted fix direction) and π[very_long→long] (suspect, shared under-tag bias) are independently estimated — asymmetry is native, not bolted on. (2) Calibrate those entries on the GOLD SET (semi-supervised: gold items pin z_i per Raykar): the gold confusion matrices empirically MEASURE that, e.g., Danbooru taggers' very_long→long error rate is high (shared bias) while their long→very_long correct-upgrade rate is the reliable signal — this turns the handoff's asserted 'direction-aware trust' into measured numbers and yields the true M4 prevalence estimate the audit couldn't get. (3) Bake asymmetric trust in as INFORMATIVE DIRICHLET PRIORS (Kim & Ghahramani): prior mass favoring acceptance of long→very_long fixes, skeptical/tight priors on very_long→long downgrades, so even thin per-source data defaults to the right asymmetric stance.

CALIBRATED CONFIDENCE → ROUTING: the aggregator's per-image POSTERIOR over the 6 classes is the routing signal. Define routes on the posterior of the GT-disagreeing class: AUTO-FIX when posterior mass on a single adjacent value (in the trusted direction) exceeds a high threshold AND item-difficulty (GLAD/minimax-entropy) is low — e.g. add very_long, keep long, honor implications; ABSTAIN when the posterior is flat or item-difficulty is high (the genuinely fuzzy long/very_long boundary — flag via CrowdTruth item-clarity), do nothing; HUMAN-REVIEW the middle band, prioritized by expected-error-mass (posterior-disagreement × frequency). Thresholds are set by gold-set ROC, not guessed. Apply the suspect direction (very_long→long downgrade) only through human review by construction (its prior/confusion makes auto-fix essentially unreachable).

ITERATION: after applying human-confirmed corrections (via the existing apply_corrections_fast.py writeback), retrain v2, add the cleaner v2 as a higher-trust source, and re-run the aggregator on the residual — the gold-set confusion matrices shrinking across rounds is the success metric (matches the handoff's iterative-denoising plan and the Raykar joint-classifier loop).

CAVEAT TO RESPECT: every method here assumes some independence; your three biggest sources (Danbooru taggers) are correlated, so the dependency modeling (DBCC/MeTaL correlation block) and the non-Danbooru VLM + community re-pull as genuinely independent sources are what actually break the systematic bias — the fusion math discounts shared error but cannot manufacture an independent signal that isn't in the inputs. Per Zheng et al. 2017, no single aggregator dominates across datasets, so empirically select among {DS, IBCC/DBCC, ordinal minimax-entropy, Raykar} ON YOUR GOLD SET before committing.

## B5: Ordinal label-noise cleaning, soft cleaned labels, iterative denoising loops that don't re-launder bias, and active-learning budget prioritization — applied to OppaiOracle's hair-length M4 (silent single-label ordinal mis-assignment) GT-cleaning pipeline.
**Bottom line:** Emit cleaned hair-length labels as SOFT ordinal distributions (SORD, Diaz & Marathe CVPR 2019; calibrate/unimodalize with ORCU 2024; represent per-sample uncertainty ORDAC-style 2025) over the carved-out ordinal sub-group — not hard flips — with adjacent-rank mass and implication parents kept hard; fix loss_functions.py:269 first so soft targets survive. Run the clean->retrain->re-clean loop as ASYMMETRIC co-teaching (Co-teaching NeurIPS 2018 / AsyCo 2023): the Danbooru-tagger consensus and the genuinely independent non-Danbooru view (VLM-assist + provenance re-pull + human gold) must AGREE before any flip; the v1/retrained model only down-weight-votes, never flips alone. It CONVERGES (vs amplifies bias) only with an IMMUTABLE gold anchor re-measured each round and a FRESH external signal injected each iteration (closed-loop self-training principle); estimate the direction-aware confusion/confident-joint (Northcutt JAIR 2021) ONLY from bias-independent signals because shared systematic bias breaks its class-conditional assumption. Spend the human budget by ranking expected-error-mass x P(mislabeled) from cross-view disagreement, de-prioritizing irreducibly-ambiguous images and protecting the rare-and-noisy medium class (Bernhardt Nature Comms 2022 + ALR MICCAI 2024), with reviewer honeypot/MCC gating retained (Mots'oehli survey 2023). Net: the handoff's instincts are right but under-formalize two things the literature pins down — the asymmetric-view requirement (the VLM oracle is the load-bearing anchor, not a footnote) and soft-ordinal label emission.
**Applicability:** CONCRETE MAPPING ONTO THE HANDOFF.

(i) HOW TO EMIT CLEANED LABELS — use SORD (Diaz & Marathe 2019) as the format, not a hard flip. For each repaired hair-length image, emit a soft distribution over the ordinal axis {very_short..absurdly_long} centered on the established value, with mass bleeding onto ADJACENT ranks proportional to phi-distance, width set from the Phase-1 gold confusion matrix. Where reviewers/consensus genuinely split (long vs very_long), widen the distribution (flat target) rather than forcing a pick — this turns 'no crisp boundary' from a bug into honest soft supervision, and stops the model memorizing coin-flips. Keep the implication-implied parents (very_long => long) as hard 1.0 positives on SEPARATE sigmoid tags; only the base-triple ordinal axis {short,medium,long} gets the SORD softening. Practically, since OppaiOracle is multi-label sigmoid (not single softmax), carve hair-length into an ordinal sub-group: either a small softmax head (cleanest, enables ORCU unimodality) or approximate soft-BCE per-tag targets (1.0 true rank, 0.1-0.3 adjacent, 0 far). The loss_functions.py:269 re-binarization constraint flagged in the handoff MUST be fixed first, or the soft targets get silently clamped back to hard. Represent residual uncertainty as ORDAC-style mean+std but DERIVE mean/std from external signals (gold + VLM + provenance), never from v1's logits.

(ii) STRUCTURE THE RETRAIN LOOP SO IT DOESN'T RE-LAUNDER BIAS — treat it as ASYMMETRIC co-teaching with an IMMUTABLE anchor, per the co-teaching + closed-loop self-training literature. Two non-correlated views: View A = Danbooru consensus (3 taggers, the convention-biased view); View B = non-Danbooru (VLM-assist + provenance re-pull + human gold). A label only flips when A and B AGREE in the high-trust asymmetric direction (GT=long -> very_long); A-only agreement (the shared-bias direction) NEVER auto-flips and goes to humans. Each round: (1) the retrained OppaiOracle may DOWN-WEIGHT-vote only, never be the sole flipper (don't feed the student its own biased pseudo-labels); (2) re-query the VLM and re-pull provenance on the NEW residual so a fresh external anchor enters every iteration; (3) re-measure the gold-set confusion matrix and STOP when the off-diagonal stops shrinking — a growing off-diagonal means the loop is amplifying, not converging. Estimate the confident-joint (Northcutt 2021) ONLY from bias-independent probabilities (gold + VLM), because shared systematic bias violates its class-conditional assumption.

(iii) SPEND THE HUMAN REVIEW BUDGET — adopt Bernhardt et al. 2022 ranking: priority = P(mislabeled) x error-mass, with P(mislabeled) from cross-view disagreement / confident-joint (NOT raw tagger confidence). De-prioritize irreducibly-ambiguous images (all signals split ~evenly, rubric can't decide) — they waste budget and maximize reviewer disagreement. Send the high-trust asymmetric direction (GT=long, View-B says very_long) FIRST for max yield. Apply ALR's imbalance fix: explicitly inject the rare-and-noisy classes (medium, very_long, absurdly_long) into the queue and PROTECT them from auto-prune, so the medium-starved corpus doesn't cause true-medium to be discarded as noise. Keep the existing screening/honeypot/MCC reviewer gating (Mots'oehli survey: active selection amplifies a noisy annotator's errors) and the operational rubric as hard preconditions.
