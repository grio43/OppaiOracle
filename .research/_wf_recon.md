## VLM assist non-Danbooru oracle at L:/Dab/VLM assist
**Verdict:** VLM assist is a genuine partial circularity-breaker: a general VLM that does not share the Danbooru annotator convention bias, and it already produced human-validated per-class precision (medium 89.3 pct, long 71.7 pct) usable as a calibration seed. But as built it is too narrow (short_hair only, 3-class not the 6-level ordinal), its confidence field is degenerate (99.98 pct high) so it cannot self-gate, and it has its OWN systematic failure mode: it overshoots up the scale (85 pct of accepted long_hair calls were really medium) and its keep verdicts are never human-checked so false negatives are unmeasured. For the best M4 strategy it should be promoted from triage afterthought to a primary independent oracle in a direction-aware fusion with the Danbooru taggers, but only after extending to all 6 levels, re-collecting a balanced human gold set across every ordinal class, and replacing the useless high/medium/low field with a calibrated abstention. The hardcoded OpenRouter API key in config.py is a live secret and must be revoked.
