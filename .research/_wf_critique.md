# Adversarial Review: Ground-Truth Cleaning Plan for M4 Ordinal Noise

I read the handoff in full and verified two load-bearing claims (the `loss_functions.py:269` alias is real; the evidence's account of the bias structure checks out). Below is the ruthless version. The plan is intellectually honest about its central risk — which is rare and commendable — but the synthesis around it has several places where the bias walks right back in through a side door, and one structural weakness that can quietly invalidate everything.

---

## 1. Every place this can still LAUNDER the systematic bias back into the GT

**1.1 The detector's trigger condition is bias-laundering by construction (the single biggest flaw).** Phase 2/§3 fires on "**≥2 of 3 Danbooru-trained taggers agree on a different adjacent value than GT**." All three taggers share the convention bias (the evidence confirms it: their consensus fires `long_hair` on 3.12M images, corpus #2 tag; `baseline_snapshot` shows `long` avg_prob 0.944 vs `very_long` 0.834 vs `medium` 0.758 — they are *quantitatively* under-confident on exactly the classes you want to recover). The direction the detector will *most reliably* fire is the **shared-bias direction** (everything → long), and the direction you actually care about (long → very_long) is exactly where all three are jointly depressed. So the detector's *recall on true M4 is suppressed by the same bias you're hunting*, while its *false-positive rate in the downgrade direction is inflated*. You have built a triage engine whose blind spot is congruent with the defect. The handoff half-acknowledges this ("partially escapes"), but then still makes tagger-consensus the corpus-scale gate that decides what humans even see. **If a true-`very_long` image is jointly called `long` by 2/3 taggers, it never enters the queue.** The bias is laundered by *omission* — the worst kind, because it's invisible in every downstream metric.

**1.2 Camie-v2 is loaded with unjustified trust.** §2a and the caveat lean on Camie-v2 as "the most valuable vote… least likely to share the under-tagging" because it's newer/long-tail-aware (IRFS). This is an *assertion*, not a measurement. Camie was still trained on Danbooru tags — IRFS rebalances *sampling frequency*, it does not re-annotate the `long`-vs-`very_long` boundary, which is an *annotation convention* problem, not a *class-frequency* problem. Long-tail sampling fixes "I rarely see `absurdly_long`," not "annotators call `very_long` hair `long`." Treating Camie as a partial bias-break without gold-set proof is precisely how correlated error gets mistaken for an independent signal. **Until the Phase-1 confusion matrix proves Camie's long→very_long off-diagonal differs structurally from WD-ViT/ML-Danbooru, it is one more correlated vote.**

**1.3 Naive consensus triple-counts correlated voters — the math the evidence flags.** "≥2 agree" treats the three taggers as independent annotators. They are positively correlated through the shared convention (CROWDLAB/Dawid-Skene caveat in the evidence: vanilla DS and majority-vote *deconvolve to the bias*, not the truth). Two correlated votes agreeing is weak evidence; the plan weights it as strong. Any fusion that doesn't model the Danbooru taggers as a single correlated block (DBCC/MeTaL dependency graph, or simply collapsing them to one effective vote) will let three echoes of one bias outvote the one genuinely independent signal.

**1.4 The iterative loop (Phase 4) is a confirmation-bias amplifier as written.** "The cleaned model becomes a better oracle next round → re-run Phase 2 on the residual." The retrained v2 is trained on GT that was *itself* shaped by the (bias-suppressed) Phase-2 triage. Feeding its predictions back as a higher-trust oracle is textbook self-training drift (evidence: closed-loop self-training amplifies unless anchored on an immutable clean set with a *fresh external signal injected every round*). The handoff's only convergence check is "track the gold-set confusion matrix shrinking" — but if the gold set is fixed and the loop overfits to *it*, the matrix shrinks while corpus error doesn't (Goodhart). **There is no rule in Phase 4 that the v2 model may only down-weight-vote and never flip alone, and no rule that the VLM/provenance must be re-queried on the new residual each round.** Both are required to keep the loop from re-laundering. Add them explicitly.

**1.5 Implication closure (Phase 4) silently reinforces the coarse default.** The M1 effort already applied `very_long_hair → long_hair` and `absurdly_long_hair → long_hair` across 5.4M images (evidence, Tag Improvement Pipeline). The handoff's Phase-4 closure pass does the same thing ("`very_long ∈ GT, long ∉ GT → add long`"). This is *correct* per the implication chain — but note what it means: the corpus is being made *maximally consistent with `long_hair` present everywhere*, which **inflates the `long_hair` prior even further** and makes the base-rate imbalance the model sees worse, not better. The closure is logically safe but statistically pours fuel on the exact distributional collapse driving M4. This isn't a reason to skip closure; it's a reason the v2 *loss* must counter the `long` base-rate (the SORD/soft-label work), and the handoff treats that as an afterthought footnote (§7) rather than a co-requirement.

**1.6 The provenance re-pull is semi-correlated and is being sold as independent.** "Current community Danbooru tags are often cleaner." Maybe — but they're *the same site's convention*. The community that wrote `long_hair` as default is the community re-pulled. Edit-recency and editor-count help (evidence), but the handoff doesn't weight by them; it just says "re-pull current tags." Current Danbooru tags are **semi-independent at best** and must be weighted as such, not treated as a clean second signal co-equal with the VLM.

## 2. Failure modes of the VLM-as-oracle path

The evidence is unusually clear here, and the handoff *under-uses* the VLM (§2a doesn't even list it; it's relegated to the existing `VLM assist` project, scoped to `short_hair` only, 3-class). Promoting it is right, but every one of these failure modes is real:

**2.1 Anime is OOD + relative-spatial measurement is the VLM's worst regime — stacked.** BLINK: GPT-4V 51%, Gemini 46% on relative-spatial vs human 96%. "Hair ends past the hips" is *exactly* a relative-spatial-landmark judgment, on a stylized domain with floating strands, occluding clothing, and exaggerated volume that has no photographic analogue. The current VLM-assist run already shows the symptom: **85% of VLM-accepted `long_hair` flags were actually `medium` on human review** — a systematic *upward* overshoot. So the VLM doesn't just have random error; it has its *own* directional bias (opposite the Danbooru one). **Mitigable, not fatal:** this is actually *useful* — a signal whose bias is anti-correlated with the Danbooru bias brackets the truth. But only if you (a) measure its per-direction confusion on gold and (b) fuse it as a *separately-calibrated* annotator, never trust its raw call. Fatal if you auto-apply VLM flags (the user must never do this; the handoff's "humans decide" rule covers it, but only if the VLM stays triage).

**2.2 VLM confidence is degenerate and unusable as a gate.** The existing run: 99.98% "high" confidence, 0 "low" (evidence). The literature confirms (Groot 2024: verbalized confidence pinned in [80,100], evidence-insensitive). **The handoff's Phase-2 "confidence-gated" flagging cannot use VLM self-reported confidence — it carries zero information.** You must replace it with *self-consistency* (sample the VLM k times at temp>0 / paraphrased prompts; agreement = the gate) and gold-set-calibrated per-direction reliability. If anyone wires the VLM's `high/medium/low` field into the auto-flag threshold, the gate is a no-op and everything passes. This is a concrete, easy-to-make fatal wiring error.

**2.3 Modality neglect — the VLM may answer from priors, not pixels.** MM-JudgeBias across 26 MLLMs documents this. For hair length the hazard is the VLM inferring length from *character archetype / caption priors* rather than measuring the visible endpoint. Mitigable: force pixel-grounded CoT ("first state where the visible hair endpoint falls relative to a named body landmark, *then* map to the class"), and ask the discrete landmark-bucket question, never a length scalar (MLLM-as-Judge: scoring unreliable, comparison reliable). The handoff's rubric supports this but doesn't *mandate* the landmark-first reasoning order in the prompt.

**2.4 Granularity collapse — the VLM is a coarse adjudicator, not a 6-level oracle.** CUB-200: ~85% coarse, ~67% fine. The existing VLM-assist was *correctly* scoped to the base triple. The synthesis pressure to "extend to all 6 levels" is half-wrong: extend the *prompt* to acknowledge 6 levels so it stops collapsing very_long into long, but **do not expect reliable very_long-vs-absurdly_long discrimination from it.** The fine end (very_long boundary) must be settled by humans informed by votes, not by the VLM. If you build the pipeline assuming a calibrated 6-class VLM posterior, you're building on sand.

**2.5 NSFW coverage hole.** 138 images blocked by provider moderation (content_filter + PROHIBITED_CONTENT), and that's at 6.83% coverage. At full corpus scale the explicit-content subset — disproportionately likely to have long/very_long hair tropes — is a *systematic* hole the VLM cannot fill, and it's exactly where the Danbooru taggers are most confident. So your one independent oracle is *missing on a non-random slice*. Mitigable only by routing the NSFW-blocked subset entirely to humans; not a reason to abandon the VLM, but a reason the coverage map must be tracked per-rating.

**2.6 Cost/coverage reality.** 6.83% in the existing run after real effort; 1.53M `short_hair` images still pending *for one tag*. The full 6-level re-run over the solo corpus is a materially larger spend. **Not fatal** (CelebA shows flag-then-verify at <1% of full-manual cost), but the plan must budget the VLM for the *triaged* subset (images where Danbooru consensus disagrees with GT *or* GT is `long`-only in the high-risk direction), not the corpus. The handoff says "flagged subset only" for provenance but is silent on VLM scoping cost.

**2.7 SECURITY — not a method risk but a live one:** the evidence reports a hardcoded OpenRouter API key committed in plaintext (`VLM assist/config.py:18`, and a second in `Tag Improvement Pipeline/config.py:93`). **Revoke both immediately, move to env vars.** This is unrelated to label quality but is a real exposure in the assets this plan reuses.

## 3. Risks that the "cleaned" GT ends up WORSE than the original

This is the section the handoff is most optimistic about, and where the danger is highest. Cleaning a 6M-image corpus with a biased triage engine can *easily* net-negative.

**3.1 Gold-set non-representativeness is the load-bearing failure (see §4).** Every confusion matrix, every trust weight, every threshold is calibrated on 200–500 images/class. If that gold set is sampled from the corpus, it inherits the medium-starvation (medium is ~6% of length-tagged images, evidence). A gold set that's mostly easy/unambiguous `long` and `short` will produce confusion matrices that look great and trust weights that are *wrong on exactly the boundary cases that matter*. The evidence is explicit: the anchor must **deliberately oversample the collapsed middle and fine end**, not sample proportionally. The handoff says "~200–500 per ordinal class" — good, that's per-class not proportional — but says nothing about sampling the *hard boundary* cases within each class. A gold set of clear-cut examples calibrates you for clear-cut images and abandons you at the boundary, which is 100% of the actual problem.

**3.2 The rubric boundaries are subjective and unstable — and the whole edifice assumes they're not.** "very_long = past hips; long = shoulder-to-hip." On a seated character? A chibi with 3-head-proportions? A character whose hips are occluded? A back-view? Floating/windswept hair? The rubric will have a *long tail of undefined cases*, and reviewers will silently resolve them differently — re-injecting exactly the structural disagreement that caused M4. The handoff calls the rubric "the single most valuable artifact" (correct) but treats writing it as a one-shot Phase-0 deliverable. **It must be a living document with an explicit "ambiguous → abstain" escape hatch and a logged exception list**, or inter-annotator agreement will quietly decay and you'll measure it too late. CrowdTruth-style item-ambiguity scoring (evidence) should drive *abstention*, not forced resolution. If the rubric forces a pick on genuinely 50/50 images, you convert "honestly uncertain" GT into "confidently wrong" GT — strictly worse.

**3.3 Over-aggressive auto-apply.** The handoff is disciplined here ("humans decide," "do not auto-apply argmax") — *except* the Phase-4 implication-closure pass, which is "separate near-auto." 99.98% safe sounds safe until you multiply by 6M: 0.02% = ~1,200 wrong auto-edits, and they're *systematic* (wherever the implication chain has a legit exception). More importantly, the FIX-UP direction (`long` → add `very_long`) is described as "the main M4 repair" and "high-trust" — there will be enormous pressure to lower the human-in-the-loop bar for it once volume is felt. **Resist.** The high-trust direction is high-trust *relative to the downgrade direction*, not high-trust in absolute terms — the VLM's 71.7% precision on the long channel (evidence) is the empirical ceiling, and that's *with* human review. Auto-applying even the trusted direction at 72% precision adds 28% noise.

**3.4 The medium_hair recovery trap — correctly identified, but the trap is deeper.** The handoff defers `medium` recovery ("every Danbooru tagger under-tags it"). Right call. But the trap has a second jaw: **medium is rare AND noisy, so a naive noisiness ranker treats true-medium as label noise and queues it for *removal/change*** (evidence, ALR). The plan's review queue is ranked by `frequency_weighted_score × group-noise-prior`. If that surfaces medium-tagged images as "suspicious," reviewers under a `long`-biased rubric may *strip* correct medium tags toward long. So deferring medium *recovery* isn't enough — you must also **protect existing `medium_hair` tags from being cleaned away**, which the plan doesn't state. Net risk: you make the medium collapse *worse*.

**3.5 Breaking implication structure.** Low risk as written (the ADD-keep-long rule is correct and repeated), but two edge cases: (a) the RESOLVE path for M2 ("keep argmax winner only if it clears its floor; else abstain") can drop a base tag that an implied modifier still depends on — e.g. resolving a `short`+`long` contradiction to `short` while `very_long_hair` is also present (a real if rare GT state) orphans the modifier. The closure pass must run *after* and re-validate, not before. (b) `apply_corrections_fast.py` "only does flat add/remove, cannot express replace" (evidence) — so every "change long→medium" becomes a remove+add pair, and if the pipeline crashes between them you get an image with *no* base length tag (a new M1). Needs transactional batching per image.

**3.6 Color groups forced exclusive.** The handoff is explicitly correct here ("do not force color groups exclusive; detect-and-abstain only; 71% hair-multi and 74% eye-multi are legit"). The risk is *scope creep*: once the ordinal machinery works, reusing it for colors by analogy will tempt someone to add a color mutex (the DataCleaning `MUTUAL_EXCLUSION_GROUPS` already has `monochrome/colorful`). **The mutex-extension instruction in §2b ("extend with the base length triple") must be fenced to length only**, or heterochromia/multi-color images get falsely flagged. This is a one-line config mistake away.

**3.7 The unmeasured false-negative floor.** The VLM auto-skips all high-confidence "keep" verdicts without human check (93,770 in the existing run, evidence) — so its *miss rate is completely unmeasured*. If the cleaned-GT success metric only counts corrections made, you're blind to the (likely large) set of true M4 errors that every signal called "keep." The corpus could be *measurably* cleaner on the reviewed slice and *unchanged* on the silent majority, and the gold-confusion-matrix metric won't catch it unless gold includes images all signals agreed to keep. **Gold must include "everyone said keep" cases**, or the success metric is self-congratulatory.

## 4. The weakest link — the single failure that invalidates everything

**The human gold set (Phase 1) is the weakest link, and its non-representativeness or under-sizing is the single failure that silently invalidates the entire effort.**

Reasoning: every quantitative claim downstream — per-direction trust weights, auto-flag thresholds, true M4 prevalence, the fusion posterior, the "is Camie actually a bias-break" question, the VLM's calibration, the iterate-loop stopping rule — is computed *against this gold set*. It is the only point in the entire chain where truth enters the system (the IDN unidentifiability theorems in the evidence, Xia/Yao, prove this is not optional: you *cannot* recover truth from the biased corpus alone; an external anchor is mathematically required). The gold set is that anchor. If it is:

- **sampled proportionally** → it inherits the medium-starvation and calibrates you to ignore medium;
- **composed of easy/clear cases** → confusion matrices look clean, trust weights are wrong at the boundary (the only place that matters), and you auto-apply with false confidence;
- **too small to estimate the rare off-diagonal cells** (the `long→very_long` and `very_long→long` cells specifically) → per-direction trust is statistical noise, and "direction-aware" becomes direction-*random*;
- **labeled against an unstable rubric** → the anchor itself carries the disagreement you're trying to eliminate, and you've calibrated to noise;
- **labeled by un-vetted reviewers** → the active-learning-amplifies-noise result (Mots'oehli, evidence) means your budget-efficient queue efficiently injects *their* bias.

And the failure is **invisible**: a bad gold set produces clean-looking confusion matrices and a shrinking gold-confusion-metric *while the corpus gets worse*, because you're measuring against the same flawed anchor that's driving the corrections (the Goodhart trap in §1.4). There is no independent tripwire in the plan that would catch "the gold set itself is biased."

**The single mitigations that matter most:** (a) size the gold set to estimate the *off-diagonal* cells, not overall accuracy — that means deliberately oversampling boundary/ambiguous images and the collapsed middle, not 200–500 random per class; (b) build a *second, held-out* gold slice the correction pipeline never sees, and measure corpus improvement against *it* (the held-out slice is your only defense against Goodharting the calibration gold); (c) include "all signals agreed keep" images in gold to measure the false-negative floor; (d) keep the screening/honeypot/MCC reviewer gating *before* gold labeling, not just for corpus review. Without (b) especially, you have no way to know if the cleaned GT is better or worse than the original.

## 5. What the handoff gets RIGHT and must be preserved

Credit where due — these are correct, hard-won, and must not be diluted in any rewrite:

1. **GT-first framing.** "Clean the labels, not the model" is correct and matches the literature (Northcutt: under high systematic-error prevalence, cleaning beats scaling; lower-capacity models can win). This justifies the v2 right-sizing decision too. Preserve it as the spine.

2. **Naming the circularity as the central design problem.** The handoff explicitly states the v1 model cannot find its own bias and forbids it as oracle (§1, §5). The IDN unidentifiability theorems (evidence) prove this is *mathematically* correct, not just prudent. This is the most important single judgment in the document.

3. **The rubric as the load-bearing artifact.** Recognizing that `long`-vs-`very_long` has *no operational definition* and that without a written one reviewers reproduce the original noise — this is exactly right (Prometheus-Vision: rubric + reference + CoT is the validated path) and is the project's highest-value output. Preserve, and make it living + abstention-capable per §3.2.

4. **Direction-aware, asymmetric trust.** Treating `long→very_long` (fix direction) and `very_long→long` (suspect/shared-bias direction) asymmetrically is correct and matches the ordinal-noise literature (asymmetric, adjacent-concentrated). The handoff *asserts* it without formalizing — the evidence supplies the formalism (per-direction confusion entries in a Dawid-Skene/IBCC backbone, calibrated on gold) — but the *instinct* is right and must survive formalization.

5. **ADD `very_long`, KEEP `long`; only the base triple is exclusive; modifiers ride implications.** Correct multi-label/WSML insight (the safe operation on multi-label GT is positive recovery, since false labels concentrate on the negative side). Preserve verbatim.

6. **Abstention-first, every-write-human-confirmed, mark low-confidence suggestions visibly.** This is the discipline that prevents §3.3. Preserve and *extend* it to the implication-closure pass (don't let "near-auto" erode it).

7. **Reusing the infrastructure, not the method.** Correctly identifies that DataCleaning's writeback (`apply_corrections_fast.py`), reviewer-QC (`screening_*`/MCC/honeypot), and the format-preserving atomic sidecar editors are the reusable backbone, while the *detection method* must be rebuilt. This matches the recon exactly (the DataCleaning ingest is single-oracle v1 judging its own GT — *not* a consensus engine, despite the misleading class names). Preserve the reuse map.

8. **Color = detect-and-abstain, never force-exclusive.** Correct, and the empirical multi-color/heterochromia rates back it. Preserve, and fence the mutex extension to length only (§3.6).

9. **Don't clean the full 6M blind; prioritize by expected error mass; log deprioritizations.** Correct budget discipline. Preserve.

### Bottom line

The handoff's *instincts* are almost all correct and its central honesty about circularity is its greatest strength. Its three exploitable weaknesses are: **(a)** it makes shared-bias Danbooru-tagger consensus the corpus-scale *gate*, so true M4 is suppressed by omission before humans ever see it — the VLM/provenance must be promoted from footnote to co-equal, gold-calibrated, correlation-aware fusion sources (the evidence's strongest and most-repeated correction); **(b)** the gold set is the unidentifiability anchor on which everything rests, yet the plan under-specifies its representativeness, off-diagonal sizing, a held-out Goodhart-defense slice, and false-negative coverage — fix this or the success metric lies; and **(c)** the iterate loop and implication-closure can re-launder/re-inflate the `long` default unless the v2 model is fenced to down-weight-voting, a fresh external signal is re-injected every round, and the v2 *loss* (soft ordinal labels; fix `loss_functions.py:269` first — confirmed a live alias) actively counters the base-rate the closure inflates. Fix those three and the plan is sound; ship it as-is and you will produce clean-looking confusion matrices over a corpus that is, on the silent majority, no better and possibly worse.
