# ADVERSARIAL REVIEW: Five Non-VLM Methods to Break the Danbooru Hair-Length Convention Bias

Corpus numbers below are taken from the project's own scans (`.research/_audit_out.json`, 44,983 solo entries; `ma_framing_scan.py`): among solo-with-length images, **medium = 2,083/35,908 ≈ 5.8%**, long_hair = 24,032, very_long = 5,814, multi-label rate = 18.48% (92% of it adjacent), implication violations ≈ 0%. These are the facts every method must survive.

## 1. Which methods secretly re-inherit the convention bias (orthogonality: proven or refuted)

The bias is **unidirectional-down, shared, low-variance, instance-dependent**. The single test that decides every method: *does its truth-injection read the minority-correct signal, or does it average over a population that all shares the down-bias?* Averaging kills variance, never shared bias (diversity-prediction theorem — correct in the evidence). Verdicts:

**M-A Geometric — ORTHOGONAL where it fires, but with a SECOND directional bias, not bias-free.** Its error source (keypoint jitter, mask boundary) is genuinely uncorrelated with the annotation convention — it never "decides long by default." BUT it is not clean: on tied/occluded hair it systematically **under-measures** (a waist-length ponytail reads short). So M-A does not re-inherit the *Danbooru* bias; it imports a *new, opposite* one. The danger is subtle: if you weight its votes symmetrically in fusion, its downward artifact on the exact long/very_long cases cancels real upgrades. It is orthogonal only if its confusion matrix is estimated **per-direction** and it abstains on tied/occluded. Verdict: orthogonal-but-second-biased. Not a launderer; a different poison.

**M-B Entity-canonical — RE-INHERITS THE BIAS in its naive (mode) form; PROVABLY orthogonal only in the directional-upper form, and even then leaks.** This is the method most likely to secretly launder. The corpus proves it: for ganyu/formidable/daiwa_scarlet/hu_tao the per-character **mode is "long" for ~every long-haired character** because the down-bias depresses very_long on *all* of a character's images simultaneously. Entity-MODE = three correlated taggers with extra steps. The evidence correctly rejects it. The directional-upper ("≥1 reliable instance asserts very_long") form IS orthogonal *in principle* — the bias structurally cannot fabricate very_long — but it has a **residual leak the evidence under-weights**: the "reliable minority" that fires very_long was itself produced by Danbooru annotators under the same convention; you are anchoring on the *tail* of the biased distribution, not on an external process. That tail is real signal (the bias can't invent very_long) but its *magnitude* is convention-shaped — heavily-fanart characters get more upgrade votes purely from volume, not from longer canonical hair. So M-B's *direction* is orthogonal but its *threshold calibration* is contaminated and must be pinned on the external gold set, not on intra-corpus rates. Verdict: conditionally orthogonal; one config flag (mode vs upper) is the difference between a bias-break and a bias-launder.

**M-C Noise-robust training — INHERITS THE BIAS FATALLY if T is self-estimated; orthogonality is entirely BORROWED from the gold set, zero intrinsic.** This is correctly diagnosed in the evidence and I confirm it is the sharpest trap. Every corpus-only transition-matrix estimator (Dual-T, VolMinNet, anchor-point, even Li 2023 multi-label-correlation) **deconvolves to the convention** because the model supplying the anchors shares it — the "consistency guarantee" then trains the bias into the v2 head *with a false proof of correctness*. The SORD/inverted-ASL pieces are differently orthogonal: they don't estimate truth, they remove the *amplification pressure* (gamma_neg=7 literally rewards reproducing the under-tag). Verdict: T-correction has **zero independent orthogonality** — it is a multiplier on whatever signal feeds it; SORD/ASL-inversion are bias-amplification-reducers, not bias-breakers.

**M-D Specialist (DINOv2 + ordinal head) — ORTHOGONAL on the backbone, but its ONLY correlated-error channel is the gold set, and that channel is wide open in two ways the evidence soft-pedals.** (i) **Gold contamination**: if reviewers eyeball existing tags while labeling, or gold is sampled proportionally (inheriting the 5.8% medium starvation), the head learns the convention directly *and the held-out metric calibrated on the same contaminated gold looks clean* — silent collapse. (ii) **Semi-supervised amplification**: the FixMatch/Noisy-Student stage (step 5) is a bias-laundering engine if the seed head has any directional skew — high-confidence pseudo-label retention will preferentially keep the confident-majority "long" calls and propagate them across 6M. Verdict: orthogonal **iff** gold is rubric-first + boundary-oversampled + held-out-Goodhart-sliced, and the semi-sup stage is gated on a held-out off-diagonal that must shrink each round or you stop. The backbone is clean; the pipeline around it is the leak.

**M-E Human-leverage — ORTHOGONAL only because a human is in the loop, but THREE of its propagation layers re-inherit the bias if mis-wired.** Clustering is orthogonal (DINOv2 geometry never saw tags). But: **entity-canonical layer = M-B, same mode-vs-upper trap**; **community re-pull is SEMI-correlated** (same site, same convention community — only the *delta* over time is orthogonal, and even that is editor-attention-driven, not guaranteed truth-ward); **active-cleaning selector inherits a biased posterior** — if the Bernhardt score is computed from tagger-consensus, the M4 mislabels sit *on the diagonal* (everyone agrees "long"), look low-noise, and **never enter the queue**. That last one is the silent killer: the method's own triage structurally cannot see the defect it targets unless the posterior is built from the orthogonal fusion. Verdict: orthogonal at the human-injection points; re-inherits at every propagation/triage point that touches Danbooru-derived signal.

**Summary of section 1:** M-C (self-T), M-B (mode), and M-E (community-as-clean-oracle, tagger-fed selector) are the three that secretly re-inherit. None of the five is *intrinsically* orthogonal end-to-end; every one routes its orthogonality through the gold set. **The gold set is the single shared point of orthogonality for all five** — which makes it the single point of failure (section 5).

## 2. Anime-domain reality check — where M-A (geometric) actually fails, and is DINOv2-encodes-hair-length wishful?

**M-A is silently wrong on the majority, and the silence is correlated with the target.** From the project's own `ma_framing_scan.py` on the longish-solo pool (n≈28,005):

- **~9.4% cleanly measurable** (full_body OR cowboy_shot AND not tied/back/seated/chibi). That is the *entire* honest operating set.
- **35.7% tied-up hair** (ponytail/twintails/bun/braid) → visible extent ≠ let-down length → pure abstain, *zero* recovery. A waist-length ponytail reads as "short."
- **~68% carry NO framing tag** → Danbooru default crops hips off-frame → the long-vs-very_long boundary (which *is* "past hips") is **geometrically undecidable** because the landmark is not in the pixels.
- The hip keypoint is the **documented weakest joint** (Khungurn 2016; Chen 2022 trained on 4K images, single-full-body-only, backward poses 5.45%). A mislocated hip flips the ratio across the exact cutoff that defines M4.

The fatal structural fact: **the abstain set anti-correlates with the defect.** Long/very_long hair is disproportionately tied up and disproportionately cropped (pin-up framing). So M-A is silent *precisely on the very_long cases the project most needs*. It is silently wrong (by abstention) on ~80%+ of the relevant pool, and that ~80% is congruent with the target, not random. Additional uncosted failures: floating strands, layered hair sandwiching the face, hair-vs-clothing color collision, second-character/held hair leaking into "lowest extent," and **chibi** (head-to-body ratio breaks the normalizer entirely). And there is **no off-the-shelf anime hair-class segmenter with published accuracy** — that sub-step is unsolved and is a multi-week build.

**Multi-character (2girls+, ~19–32% of corpus):** M-A cannot attribute a hair mask to a skeleton reliably; M-B/M-E cannot attribute a length tag to a character. The whole multi-subject slice is a coverage hole for the geometric and entity methods alike.

**Is "DINOv2 encodes anime hair-length" wishful? PARTLY.** The honest evidence (DACoN 2025: frozen DINOv2 57.5% zero-shot on anime line-art part-matching, beating CLIP 36.7%) supports that DINOv2 encodes **part-level structure** on line art — enough to *cluster* and to separate the **base triple** {short/medium/long} as a part-extent signal. It does **not** establish that DINOv2 linearly separates the **long-vs-very_long boundary**, which is the actual defect — that is a relative-spatial measurement (hair endpoint vs body landmark) and DACoN itself names occlusion and extreme pose as DINO failure modes. So: DINOv2-for-base-triple is *documented*; DINOv2-for-the-M4-fine-boundary is *wishful* and must be treated as soft/abstain. **CLIP is worse** (36.7% in the same benchmark) — using CLIP as the clustering substrate is a measurable downgrade; prefer DINOv2.

## 3. The hardest cell: MEDIUM (~5.8%, eaten from both sides). Who recovers it, who worsens it?

This is where four of five methods fail and the review must be blunt. Medium is collapsed (`_audit_out.json`: 2,083 vs long 24,032; long:medium ≈ 11.5:1) and eaten **bidirectionally** (short→medium *and* medium→long), which is structurally harder than M4's unidirectional long→very_long.

- **M-A geometric — CANNOT touch medium.** short-vs-medium is a head-to-shoulder/collarbone distinction *below the spatial resolution of a COCO-17 skeleton on stylized art*. Explicitly out.
- **M-B entity-canonical — DOES NOTHING for medium and can WORSEN it.** Medium is rarely a character's *canonical* value (it's an in-between most designs don't sit at), and the bias is bidirectional so the "directional-upper" trick has no clean direction to exploit. Worse: if you ever run the *downgrade* propagation (long-canonical character → strip medium on a deviating image), you erase legitimate medium. M-B must be **fenced to the upgrade direction only**, leaving medium untouched.
- **M-C noise-robust — UNDER-CORRECTS medium structurally.** A gold set sampled proportionally has ~5.8% medium → too few examples to estimate medium's transition cell stably → the correction leaves medium collapsed. Only fixable by **deliberately oversampling medium in gold** (which the synthesis already mandates) AND treating short→medium and medium→long as *two separate* cells. Even then, SORD helps (makes short↔medium↔long adjacency cheap) but cannot *invent* the missing medium positives.
- **M-D specialist — THE ONLY METHOD THAT CAN ACTUALLY RECOVER MEDIUM, and only if gold oversamples it.** A DINOv2 base-triple head trained on a *boundary-and-medium-oversampled* gold set is the one mechanism here that perceives the short/medium/long distinction from non-Danbooru features and can fire medium where GT omitted it. This is medium's best hope. The catch: granularity collapse (CUB fine ~67%) means it will be noisy at short↔medium, and if gold is proportional the head never learns medium at all — so M-D recovers medium **iff active-learning (BADGE/core-set) deliberately mines medium-boundary cases**, which is exactly the regime AL targets.
- **M-E human-leverage — RECOVERS MEDIUM but ONLY through direct human labels, and its automation actively ENDANGERS medium.** The Bernhardt noisiness ranker flags rare true-medium as "suspicious," and a reviewer under a long-default rubric strips it toward long → **worsens the collapse**. The ALR rare-class injection (Bhattacharya 2024) and the non-negotiable "protect existing medium_hair from removal" are the only guards. Net: M-E can recover medium at the cost of human minutes, and will *destroy* medium if the rare-class protection is omitted.

**Section 3 verdict:** Only **M-D (specialist) + M-E (human gold)** can recover medium, and both require medium-oversampled gold. M-A and M-B are out; M-C only stops the bleeding, doesn't refill. The non-negotiable "defer medium recovery on Danbooru-only signals" is correct — every Danbooru-derived input (including M-B and the tagger-fed parts of M-E) under-tags medium, so medium recovery is **gold-and-specialist-bound or it does not happen.**

## 4. Noise-robust training: does it satisfy the user, and where does T break?

**Does fixing model behavior without cleaning GT satisfy the user? NO — it is a category mismatch with the stated goal, and this is the most important strategic finding.** The user's goal is explicitly to **clean the poison GT itself** and use the model as a *suggestion engine*. M-C does the opposite: it leaves the 6M poisoned sidecars on disk and trains a v2 whose *predictions* are de-biased. Consequences:

1. The on-disk GT is still poison. Any future re-train, any export, any downstream consumer that reads sidecars inherits the bias. M-C does not produce a clean dataset; it produces a clean-ish model over a dirty dataset.
2. As a **suggestion engine**, a SORD/ASL-corrected v2 will *suggest* very_long more often — which is what the user wants behaviorally — but it will suggest it as a **soft distribution**, and the user's downstream apply step still needs a *decision*. M-C hands the decision back unsolved on exactly the fuzzy boundary.
3. The Goodhart trap: forward correction makes v2 predict the **gold-calibrated rate**. If the gold rate is mis-estimated, v2 confidently predicts a *wrong corpus-wide distribution* and you cannot see it without a held-out gold slice the correction never touched.

So M-C is **not a substitute for cleaning** — it is a *complement* that spends the same gold set across 6M to fix the suggestion engine, covering the silent majority cleaning never reaches. It satisfies the "better suggestions" half of the goal and **fails the "clean the GT" half entirely.** It must be sold as the corpus-wide *training* fix that runs *after* the cleaning track produces the gold + high-confidence corrections, never as the cleaning itself.

**Where transition-matrix estimation breaks for instance-dependent (not class-conditional) noise — precisely:** Patrini's forward/backward correction is consistent only under **class-conditional** noise: P(noisy | true, x) = P(noisy | true). M4 violates this **twice**:

- **Instance-dependence:** the long/very_long boundary is a *feature-dependent perceptual judgment* — P(omit very_long | true=very_long) depends on the image (visible hips? tied up? pose?), not just on the class. A single global T over-corrects easy standing-hair-down images and under-corrects occluded boundary images. Xia 2020 (part-dependent-T) and Yao 2021 (IDN under SCM) **prove instance-dependent T is unidentifiable without extra structure** — the formal statement that a corpus-self-estimated T for M4 is *mathematically invalid*, not just noisy.
- **Systematic directionality:** the noise is false-negative-only (omit very_long, never invent it), so corpus estimators that assume some symmetric structure deconvolve to the convention.

The escape (the only one): estimate **one dominant off-diagonal cell** (rho10 for very_long, secondarily the two medium cells) **from the external gold set**, condition the very_long flip on long-present (reduces instance-dependence to a sub-population), accept it corrects the **aggregate rate not per-image**, and use T-Revision to refine. Even then it is "consistent for the *rate*," not "correct per image" — which is acceptable *only* because the user wants suggestions, not because the math is satisfied.

## 5. The weakest link of the BEST combination, and the single failure that invalidates it

**Best combination (justified in section 6):** **Gold set (rubric-first, boundary+medium-oversampled, held-out Goodhart slice)** → **M-B directional-upper** for the long→very_long character-head first pass → **M-D specialist** for medium recovery + the characterless residual + corpus-scale leverage → **M-E human-leverage** routing the queue and adjudicating → **M-C (SORD + inverted-ASL + gold-anchored single-cell T)** as the v2 corpus-wide training fix. M-A is a *narrow optional confirmer* on the ~9% clean slice, not load-bearing.

**The weakest link is the GOLD SET — specifically its representativeness and rubric stability.** Every method in the combination routes its orthogonality, every threshold, every trust weight, every transition cell, and every stopping rule through it. It is the *only* place real truth enters and the *only* shared orthogonality anchor (section 1).

**The single failure that invalidates the entire combination:** **a gold set that is itself sampled under the convention** — i.e., reviewers anchored on existing tags, OR proportional sampling that inherits the 5.8% medium starvation and the long-only majority, OR an unstable/undefined long-vs-very_long rubric that forces reviewers to reproduce the original coin-flip. If this happens:

- M-B's upper-threshold calibrates to the biased tail → launders.
- M-D's head learns the convention from contaminated gold → orthogonality collapses **silently**, because the held-out metric is calibrated on the same contaminated gold and **looks clean**.
- M-C's T off-diagonal is the biased rate → forward correction trains the bias into v2 *with a false consistency proof*.
- M-E's selector posterior is biased → the M4 mislabels stay on the diagonal and never enter the queue.

All four fail *invisibly and simultaneously*, and you ship a v2 that confidently reproduces the bias over a corpus you believe you cleaned. This is the Goodhart catastrophe. The only defenses are the ones the synthesis already names and must be treated as **non-negotiable, not best-effort**: (a) rubric-first labeling with reference images, never tag-anchored; (b) oversample the boundary and the collapsed medium (do NOT sample proportionally); (c) a **second held-out gold slice the pipeline never sees**, the sole Goodhart defense; (d) an "all-signals-said-keep" subset to measure the false-negative floor; (e) reviewer honeypot/MCC gating before any gold is trusted. Miss any one of these and the combination is worse than doing nothing, because it manufactures false confidence.

**Second weakest link (named for completeness):** M-E's active-cleaning selector being fed a tagger-derived posterior — if wired that way, the defect is on the diagonal and never queued. Mitigation: the selector posterior MUST come from the orthogonal fusion (M-B upper + M-D + gold), never from tagger consensus.

## 6. Ranking — genuinely strongest method / minimal combination

**No single method is sufficient.** Every one routes orthogonality through the gold set, and each covers a different, non-overlapping slice of the defect. Ranked by independent value-per-cost, ruthlessly:

**1. M-D Specialist (DINOv2 + CORN/SORD ordinal head on rubric-first gold) — STRONGEST.** Its only correlated-error channel (gold) is the one thing you fully control; its backbone is documented to carry part-level anime semantics (DACoN); it is the **only method that can recover medium**; it reuses the gold the project must build anyway (≈zero marginal label cost); frozen backbone makes it cheap (cache embeddings once); and it scales to 6M via semi-sup *after* proving out on held-out gold. Ceiling: reliable on the base triple, honestly-soft on the long/very_long fine boundary (correct behavior, not failure). It is the best *single* lever and the corpus-scale workhorse.

**2. M-E Human-leverage — STRONGEST for the corpus HEAD and the only place real truth enters.** Puts the bias-independent human exactly where redundancy can't help, amortized via entity + cluster + community-delta across millions of images (~150–400 human-hours for the head vs >25 person-years naive). It is less a "method" than the *delivery system* for the gold set and the human adjudication that M-D, M-B, and M-C all depend on. Ranked just below M-D only because three of its layers re-inherit the bias if mis-wired (section 1) and it is human-bound, not autonomous.

**3. M-B Entity-canonical (directional-upper ONLY) — STRONG, CHEAP, NARROW first pass.** Near-zero per-image cost, covers the character-tagged majority (~62%+) for the long→very_long upgrade, GT-only (immune to all VLM/anime-perception failure). But: mode-form launders the bias (reject it), does nothing for medium, fails on OCs (~13%)/variable-hair/small-bags/multi-char, and its threshold needs external (gold) calibration. A precise high-trust *annotator* in the fusion, not a solution.

**4. M-C Noise-robust training — STRONG COMPLEMENT, ZERO standalone bias-break.** The cheap pieces (invert gamma_neg=7→~0-2 on the fine-end length tags per SPLC/Hill; add SORD ordinal soft head; **fix `loss_functions.py:269` first** or every soft label is silently re-binarized) are do-this-first, domain-agnostic, and stop the v2 from *amplifying* the bias. But T-correction has zero intrinsic orthogonality and **does not clean the GT** (section 4) — it borrows the gold's orthogonality to fix the suggestion engine corpus-wide. Run it last, as the v2 training fix that consumes the cleaning track's output.

**5. M-A Geometric — WEAKEST; a narrow optional confirmer, not a primary lever.** Genuinely orthogonal where it fires, but the corpus kills it: ~9% measurable, ~80% abstain, and the abstention anti-correlates with the very_long target. Cannot touch medium. No off-the-shelf anime hair segmenter (multi-week build) for a signal covering ~10% of the defect. Deploy *only* as a high-precision third annotator on the clean full-body/hair-down slice — and only after gold + rubric exist (it depends on them for R-cutoffs and the abstention gate). When it *agrees* with the opposite-biased VLM on the clean slice, that cross-family agreement is strong enough to auto-upgrade — that is its one high-value use. Build it last, or not at all if budget is tight.

**Minimal viable combination (drop everything else):** **Rubric-first held-out gold** + **M-D specialist** (medium recovery + corpus scale) + **M-E human routing** (head cleaning + adjudication) + **M-C cheap loss fixes** (SPLC/SORD/`loss_functions.py:269`). M-B is a cheap accelerant you add because it's nearly free; M-A is optional. The load-bearing dependency of all of it is the gold set, and the single failure that invalidates it is a convention-contaminated gold (section 5).

**The one-line truth:** there is no non-VLM method that breaks the bias by itself — all five are *transducers that spend an external gold anchor across the corpus*, and the entire program lives or dies on whether that gold is sampled by rubric or by convention.
