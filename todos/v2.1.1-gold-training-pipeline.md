## 0. IS / IS NOT

**IS:** a full training run on gold + gold-augmented data, producing the next checkpoint with cleaner boundaries on the noisy axes; gated by a sealed clean/real eval slice + off-diagonal metric (§3, §4) — noisy-val F1 cannot see the over-fire.

**IS NOT:**
- ❌ Data-cleaning / label-correction or a review/suggestion UI — that is the cleaning pipeline (janitor plan).
- ❌ A frozen-backbone head-only (DFR) refit — this is a *full* run. (Janitor §3.3 **is** a DFR refit over the same gold; do not conflate the two programs.)
- ❌ A fine-tune of the biased V2 checkpoint on the small gold set — the documented "trap" (Kumar et al. 2022, arXiv:2202.10054): feature distortion happens "even with an infinitesimally small learning rate", so a low LR does not make it safe. See §5, §7.

**Diagonal blind spot** (*don't let the tagger's own probability decide its own labels are wrong*, golden_set_plan §2.2/§8) holds by construction: V2.1.1 only trains; wrongness is decided upstream (external anchor + human, janitor plan). V2.1.1 consumes cleaned output, never certifies it.

## 1. Data flow

The cleaning pipeline (janitor plan) turns raw booru + Anima gold into corrected labels via external anchor + human confirm/reject. V2.1.1 merges the corrected Anima into the data-limited main pool (filling starved cells), runs a full training pass, and evaluates the resulting checkpoint with the off-diagonal metric (§4) on the **sealed REAL-only slice of janitor §3.2** — frozen before Stage A, disjoint from everything promoted into training (§3).

Anima is **TUNE-only** (janitor §3.1, user decision Rev 3): it appears in no eval slice, no calibration set, no gate, and no certification set. It is screened by Gemma 4 31B IT, not human-reviewed, so ε_gold must be measured and Anima never certifies anything (v2-plan §9.4: the Anima synthetic set is never the arbiter).

**Non-negotiables baked into the flow:**
1. **Eval on the sealed REAL-only slice, never on Anima** — otherwise there is no yardstick and V2.1.1 is unfalsifiable. Rationale, sizing and disjointness rules: janitor §3.2.
2. **Watch the synthetic:real ratio.** The gold is Anima (ComfyUI) output; the deployment domain is real booru art. Promoting too much synthetic into training installs a synthetic-domain bias. Quantify the ratio before committing (§7). *(Unique to this doc — v2-plan §9.4 forbids Anima as the arbiter but says nothing about the training ratio.)*
3. **Prior-correct before trusting the operating point** — boundary-**oversampled** gold ≠ deployment prior (§6; rationale golden_set_plan §2.3).

## 2. The boundary problem

`absurdly_long_hair` has only **n=143** positives (the chain `absurdly ⇒ very_long ⇒ long` holds at 0.0% violation in-data) and the head is independent sigmoids with zero rank coupling, so the detector latches onto generic "very long hair" features and fires across the long population. Gold training supplies many clean rare positives plus clean sibling negatives — a genuine strength for exactly this defect — but it *softens*, never enforces, `P(absurdly) ≤ P(very_long) ≤ P(long)`; the ordinal head stays deferred (§5 P2).

Full audit, the supporting numbers, and the refutation of hierarchy-coherence (C-HMCNN) and CRM as fixes: _wf5_v2.1.1_verification.md §3.

## 4. Off-diagonal confusion metric — spec owned here  🔴 blocking

**Status: partially instrumented (aggregate margin only).** `asl_telemetry.py` already ships a val-side **sibling-gap** metric: confusable groups are loaded from `configs/confusable_groups.json` (present: `hair_color`, `eye_color`, `hair_length`, `breast_size`) and, over val rows with **exactly one** labeled group member, it computes `mean(labeled_sibling_prob − max unlabeled_sibling_prob)`, emitted as `asl_val/sibling_gap/<name>` and `asl_val/sibling_gap_macro` (§8). That is an **aggregate group margin**: it says the group boundary moved, not which cell fires on which. (v2-plan Appendix A also lists an Anima recall canary — explicitly "probe, not anchor".)

**Net-new work — per-cell conditional fire-rate.** Probability / fire-rate of tag *j* on the subset where GT tag *i* is present, for target cells **`long↔very_long↔absurdly`, `aqua↔blue↔green`, `grey↔white`** — a masked column-subset over cached val probs/targets (reuse tools/find_pr_threshold.py `probs_cpu`/`targs_cpu`), reported per-checkpoint on the sealed slice. Cheapest place to build it: the same val-side chunk loop that computes sibling-gap, driven off the same `confusable_groups.json`.

**Ownership:** the mechanics exist only here, so **§4 is the canonical spec**. Janitor §3.3 consumes this metric as its per-group gate and needs it built first (janitor's prerequisite list points back at this section — that is the direction of the pointer, not a loop). Gate-3 reporting per v2-plan §9.3.

## 5. Training mechanics & guardrails  🔴

- [ ] **Pick the training regime (§7).** Default: from scratch (or continue) on the FULL augmented corpus, *not* fine-tune-on-gold (the §0 trap).
- [ ] **Rare-class handling.** If promoted gold doesn't balance rare cells: decoupled classifier rebalancing (cRT, Kang 2020, arXiv:1910.09217) and/or logit adjustment (Menon 2021, arXiv:2007.07314) — both config-only compatible; keep ASL `gamma_neg`/`gamma_pos` config-driven.
- [ ] **Fix the re-binarize snapshot BEFORE any soft/ordinal label (§8).** Targets are snapshotted *before* smoothing for the focal weight masks, so a soft target (e.g. 0.7) would leak into *both* the positive and negative masks. Correct for {0,1}; a latent bug the moment soft labels arrive.
- [ ] **No-regression gate.** Compare the ~19,250 non-targeted tags' sealed-slice mAP against the prior checkpoint. A full run *can* regress them — janitor's column-masked head refit gets no-regression by construction, a full run does not. A real gate, not a formality.

**Augmentation routing:** V2 §7 routes letterbox translation jitter and downscale-only
scale jitter here by name. They are **not adopted** into this full training run.
[The consolidated augmentation record](v2-augmentation.md) carries their status; round 2
recommends parking both rather than bundling an unmeasured augmentation change into the
boundary-improvement comparison. Any separate augmentation study needs its own explicit
scope decision; routing alone does not authorize an experiment arm.

### P2 — ordinal structure (DEFERRED — the real cure for the hair-length axis)  🟡
- CORN/CORAL sub-head over the length/breast groups enforcing `P(≥long) ≥ P(≥very_long) ≥ P(≥absurdly)` (CORAL arXiv:1901.07884; CORN arXiv:2111.08851); the SORD soft-label path needs the §8 re-binarize fix first.
- Source ordinal gold from **real human-rated booru**, not only synthetic (golden_set_collection_targets §8).
- Recommendation: keep ordinal machinery out of V2.1.1 — cleaner data this round, structural head later.

## 6. Prior-shift / threshold correction  🟡

Gold is boundary-oversampled ≠ deployment booru prior; uncorrected, the model over-fires the oversampled classes at a fixed threshold (golden_set_plan §2.3). A per-tag threshold **loader** exists but **no producer** derives prior-shift offsets — the current calibrator fits P=R on the *biased* val split (§8).

- [ ] Emit per-tag **prior-shift offsets** into `thresholds.json`. The deployment prior is **known**, so use the **closed-form analytic offset** `−log P_gold(y) + log P_booru(y)` (Saerens 2002; Menon 2021) — **not BBSE** (Lipton 2018 estimates an *unknown* prior; its error blows up on exactly the rare cells). Keep a tunable temperature fitted on the sealed slice (synthetic≠real ⇒ partial label-shift). For one-vs-all sigmoids the offset is exactly a per-tag threshold shift (sigmoid monotonicity): raising `absurdly_long_hair`'s threshold toward its ~0.3% prior directly suppresses the residual over-suggestion — symptom relief, not a cure.
- [ ] **Sequencing against the cleaning pipeline.** If cleaning runs first, the janitor §4.2 **ρ-corrected** target prior supersedes the naive corpus prior *and* janitor §4.2 demotes the closed-form offset itself to initialization/diagnostic only. So: cleaning-first ⇒ ρ-corrected prior is authoritative, the analytic offset is only the initializer; V2.1.1-first ⇒ the analytic offset is the producer and must be revisited once ρ̂ exists.

## 9. Acceptance criteria
- **Yardstick exists:** off-diagonal confusion is measurable per cell on a sealed slice nothing trained on (§3, §4).
- **Targeted boundaries improved:** `long→absurdly` fire-rate and the color confusion cells shrink on the sealed slice vs the prior checkpoint.
- **No broad regression:** the ~19,250 non-targeted tags' sealed-slice mAP is not worse than the prior checkpoint.
- **Prior-corrected:** operating point matches the real-booru prior, not the gold prior (§6).
- **Domain honesty:** the synthetic:real training ratio is recorded and within an acceptable bound (§7).
