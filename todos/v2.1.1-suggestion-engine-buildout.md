# V2.1.1 — Gold-Trained Tagger (training-only pipeline)

**Date:** 2026-06-14
**Status:** Build-out plan. **Rewritten 2026-06-14** to reflect the actual scope (see §0): V2.1.1 is a *training* pipeline, not a suggestion/cleaning tool. Cleaning + review live in a separate pipeline (`Pipeline2_review`).

**Goal.** V2.1.1 is a **full training run** of the tagger on the **gold / gold-augmented dataset**. The corrected synthetic Anima images are promoted into the main training pool (which is data-limited, especially in the starved cells), and V2.1.1 trains on the result. Its job is to **move the model's decision boundaries** — shrink the off-diagonal confusions baked in by dataset noise (e.g. `long_hair → absurdly_long_hair` over-firing; `aqua ↔ blue ↔ green`) — by learning from cleaner, better-balanced data.

It is **not** a data-cleaning program, a label proposer, or a human-review front-end. Those are a separate pipeline.

> **Scope change from the previous draft.** The earlier version framed V2.1.1 as a human-in-the-loop "suggestion engine" that surfaces removal/addition candidates for human confirmation and never auto-writes. That responsibility now belongs to **`Pipeline2_review`** (the dedicated cleaning/review pipeline + the existing `DataCleaning Project` writeback). The cleaning-side workstreams that used to live here — external-anchor Confident Learning detect-and-remove, the missing-positive proposer, and the review UI — have moved out (§8). The verified research for them is preserved in [_wf5_v2.1.1_verification.md](../.research/_wf5_v2.1.1_verification.md) so it can be handed to that pipeline.

---

## 0. What V2.1.1 IS and IS NOT

**IS:**
- A **full training run** of the tagger on the **gold + gold-augmented corpus** (corrected Anima merged into the main pool).
- The pipeline that produces the next checkpoint with (hopefully) cleaner boundaries on the noisy axes.
- Gated by a **trustworthy yardstick**: a held-out clean/gold eval slice with an **off-diagonal confusion metric**, because today's noisy-val F1 cannot see the over-fire we're trying to kill (§3, §4).

**IS NOT:**
- ❌ A data-cleaning / label-correction program — that is **`Pipeline2_review`**.
- ❌ A human-review / suggestion UI — also `Pipeline2_review`.
- ❌ A **frozen-backbone head-only (DFR) refit** — V2.1.1 is a *full* training run, not a last-layer patch.
- ❌ A **fine-tune of an existing biased V2 checkpoint on the small gold set** — that is the documented "trap" (feature distortion, Kumar et al. 2022, ICLR, arXiv:2202.10054). See §5 / the open decision in §7 about which training regime this is.

**Relationship to the cleaning pipeline (`Pipeline2_review`).** The "diagonal blind spot" non-negotiable — *don't let the tagger's own probability decide its own labels are wrong* (golden_set_plan §2.2/§8) — is satisfied **by construction** here: V2.1.1 only *trains*; the decision that a label was wrong is made upstream in `Pipeline2_review` (external anchor + human). V2.1.1 consumes the cleaned output; it never certifies it.

## 1. Data flow

```
[Pipeline2_review]                         [V2.1.1 — this pipeline]
 raw booru + Anima gold                     main pool (data-limited)
   │  external-anchor cleaning                     ▲
   │  + human confirm/reject                        │  merge corrected Anima
   ▼                                                │  (fills starved cells)
 corrected labels ──────────────────────────────────┘
                                                     │
                          ┌──────────────────────────┴───────────┐
                          │  HOLD OUT a disjoint clean/gold slice │  (never trained on)
                          ▼                                       ▼
                    TRAIN (full run) ───► checkpoint ───► EVAL on held-out slice
                                                          (off-diagonal metric, §4)
```

**Non-negotiables baked into the flow:**
1. **Hold out a disjoint clean slice BEFORE training.** Most corrected Anima is promoted into training, but a fixed slice must stay sealed for eval — otherwise there is no yardstick and V2.1.1 is unfalsifiable (this was V1's exact failure: model selection on noisy `val/f1_macro`).
2. **Watch the synthetic:real ratio.** The gold is Anima (ComfyUI) output; the deployment domain is real booru art. Promoting too much synthetic into training installs a synthetic-domain bias. Quantify the ratio before committing (§7).
3. **Prior-correct before trusting the operating point.** The gold is boundary-**oversampled** ≠ deployment prior; correct per-tag thresholds or you swap one directional bias for the opposite (§6).

## 2. The boundary problem this is meant to fix (grounded)

The motivating defect: on `GT=long_hair` images the model assigns **high probability to `absurdly_long_hair`** (a false-positive "suggestion" in the review tool, which surfaces raw model probabilities). The audit ([.research/_audit_out.json](../.research/_audit_out.json), 60k sampled / ~45k solo) explains why:

- `absurdly_long_hair` occurs only **n=143**; the chain `absurdly ⇒ very_long ⇒ long` is respected in the data at **0.0% violation** (all 143 co-occur with both parents).
- The head is **independent sigmoids with zero rank coupling** ([model_architecture.py:407](../model_architecture.py#L407)); with configured `gamma_neg=7` ([configs/unified_config.yaml:297](../configs/unified_config.yaml#L297) — note: the *source* default at [loss_functions.py:40-41](../loss_functions.py#L40-L41) is 3.0/1.0, so say "configured", not "default"), the `absurdly_long` detector trains on ~143 positives vs ~45k negatives and latches onto generic "very long hair" features.

**How a gold-trained V2.1.1 attacks this — and its ceiling.** Training on corrected, prompt-controlled Anima can supply **many clean `absurdly_long` positives** plus clean `very_long`/`long` negatives, directly relieving the n=143 rarity that drives the over-fire — this is a genuine strength of the gold-training approach for *this* defect. **But** the head still has no ordinal structure, so cleaner data softens the boundary without enforcing `P(absurdly) ≤ P(very_long) ≤ P(long)`. The structural fix (ordinal head) remains a later iteration (§5, P2). Set expectations accordingly: more-clean-data helps; it is not the full cure.

> A hierarchy-coherence / monotonicity constraint at inference does **not** help here and should not be added as a fix: the data is already 100% coherent (nothing to repair), and the canonical module (C-HMCNN, Giunchiglia & Lukasiewicz 2020, NeurIPS, arXiv:2010.10151) enforces coherence *upward* (raises the parent), which cannot lower an over-confident child. Coherence is necessary but not sufficient. (Full reasoning: [_wf5_v2.1.1_verification.md](../.research/_wf5_v2.1.1_verification.md) §3.)

## 3. Prerequisite — held-out clean/gold eval slice  🔴 blocking

Today there is no trustworthy slice: [create_dataloaders](../dataset_loader.py#L2463) auto-splits **95/5 from the same noisy corpus** ([dataset_loader.py:2630-2641](../dataset_loader.py#L2630-L2641)), and model selection / early-stop runs on noisy `val/f1_macro` ([train_direct.py:2339-2343](../train_direct.py#L2339-L2343)) — where an n=143 class contributes ~nothing to the macro average. `val/mAP` is computed but **not** the selection metric ([train_direct.py:2160](../train_direct.py#L2160)).

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Add an optional **clean/gold eval directory** parameter to the data loader — do **not** route it through the 95/5 random split.
- [ ] Move best-metric / early-stop bookkeeping to **`val/mAP` on the clean slice** (threshold-free; already planned in [progressive-training-plan.md §6](progressive-training-plan.md#L337-L341)).
- [ ] Lock the eval slice as **disjoint** from anything promoted into training (see §1 non-negotiable #1).

**Done when:** training selects checkpoints on a clean, threshold-free metric over a slice nothing trained on.

## 4. Prerequisite — off-diagonal confusion metric  🔴 blocking (this is how you SEE the win)

`P(absurdly_long predicted | GT long_hair)` is **not computable today** — every per-tag stat in [evaluation_metrics.py](../evaluation_metrics.py) and [validation_loop.py](../validation_loop.py) is **diagonal** (tag *i* vs its own GT column). Without this you cannot tell whether V2.1.1 shrank the over-fire or just moved aggregate F1.

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Add a **per-tag off-diagonal metric**: predicted probability / fire-rate of tag *j* on the subset where `GT tag i` is present, for the target cells (`long↔very_long↔absurdly`, `aqua↔blue↔green`, `grey↔white`). Small add — a masked column-subset over already-cached val probs/targets (reuse [find_pr_threshold.py](../tools/find_pr_threshold.py)'s `probs_cpu`/`targs_cpu`).
- [ ] Report it per-checkpoint so a clean round's effect on the *targeted* confusion is visible.

**Done when:** for any checkpoint you can read, on the sealed slice, the `long→absurdly` fire-rate and the color confusion cells.

## 5. Training mechanics & guardrails  🔴

**Tasks / decisions:**
- [ ] **Pick the training regime (§7 open decision).** Default recommendation: train **from scratch (or continue on the FULL augmented corpus)**, *not* fine-tune the biased V2 checkpoint on gold only — Kumar et al. 2022 (ICLR, arXiv:2202.10054) show feature distortion happens *"even with an infinitesimally small learning rate"*, so a low LR does not make a small-gold fine-tune safe.
- [ ] **Rare-class handling.** If the promoted gold doesn't fully balance the rare cells, consider decoupled representation/classifier rebalancing (Kang et al. 2020 cRT, ICLR, arXiv:1910.09217) and/or logit adjustment (Menon et al. 2021, ICLR, arXiv:2007.07314) — both consistent with the config-only ASL decision (no loss rewrite). Keep ASL `gamma_neg`/`gamma_pos` config-driven.
- [ ] **Fix the re-binarize snapshot BEFORE any soft/ordinal label.** [loss_functions.py:269](../loss_functions.py#L269) (`targets_for_focal = targets`) snapshots targets before smoothing; a soft target (e.g. 0.7) would leak into *both* the positive and negative focal weights. Correct for plain {0,1}; a latent bug the moment soft labels are introduced.
- [ ] **No-regression check.** Compare the ~19,250 non-targeted tags' clean-slice mAP against the prior checkpoint — a full train *can* regress them (unlike the old frozen-backbone framing), so this is a real gate now, not a formality.

### P2 — ordinal structure (DEFERRED — the real cure for the hair-length axis)  🟡
The independent-sigmoid head cannot enforce the length ordering. When tackled (later iteration):
- [ ] Add a **CORN/CORAL sub-head** over the length/breast groups (CORAL: Cao et al. 2020, Pattern Recognition Letters, arXiv:1901.07884; CORN: Shi et al. 2021, arXiv:2111.08851) enforcing `P(≥long) ≥ P(≥very_long) ≥ P(≥absurdly)`.
- [ ] Add a soft-label path (SORD: Diaz & Marathe 2019, CVPR) — **requires the [loss_functions.py:269](../loss_functions.py#L269) fix first**.
- [ ] Source ordinal gold from **real human-rated booru**, not only synthetic (synthetic is weaker on the subtle ordinal boundaries; [golden_set_collection_targets §8](../.research/golden_set_collection_targets.md#L189-L191)).

**Recommendation:** keep the ordinal *machinery* out of V2.1.1; rely on cleaner/balanced data this round, add the structural head later.

## 6. Prior-shift / threshold correction  🟡
The gold is boundary-**oversampled** ≠ deployment booru prior. Without correction, a gold-trained model over-fires the oversampled classes at a fixed threshold and installs the opposite directional bias ([golden_set_plan §2.3](../.research/golden_set_plan.md#L66-L71)).

**Code reality:** there is a per-tag threshold **loader** ([onnx_infer.py:298](../onnx_infer.py#L298) reads `thresholds.json`) but **no producer** that derives prior-shift offsets; [find_pr_threshold.py](../tools/find_pr_threshold.py) calibrates P=R on the *biased* val split.

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Emit per-tag **prior-shift offsets** into `thresholds.json`. Because the deployment booru prior is **known**, use the **closed-form analytic offset** `−log P_gold(y) + log P_booru(y)` (Saerens et al. 2002; Menon et al. 2021) — **not BBSE** (Lipton et al. 2018 estimates an *unknown* prior from unlabeled data; its error blows up on exactly the rare cells here). Keep a tunable temperature on the clean slice (synthetic≠real ⇒ partial label-shift).
- [ ] For one-vs-all sigmoids this offset is exactly a per-tag decision-threshold shift — so raising `absurdly_long_hair`'s threshold toward its ~0.3% prior also directly suppresses the residual over-suggestion (symptom relief, not a cure).

**Done when:** the operating point matches the real-booru prior, not the gold prior.

## 8. Out of scope — handed to `Pipeline2_review`
These were workstreams in the prior draft; they belong to the dedicated cleaning/review pipeline, **not** V2.1.1. Their verified research (and the caveats below) is preserved in [_wf5_v2.1.1_verification.md](../.research/_wf5_v2.1.1_verification.md):

Caveats for that pipeline: CL's class-conditional assumption breaks on instance-dependent/adjacent ordinal noise (keep CL's *ordering*, discard its noise-rate estimate; ~51% precision → human gate; Northcutt et al. 2021, arXiv:2103.14749; Thyagarajan et al. 2022 multi-label, arXiv:2211.13895 — fit a multi-output probe, not one-vs-rest). - **Missing-positive (M1) proposer** — teacher-tagger additions; off-diagonal, blind-spot-safe.
- **Human review UI + writeback** — confirm/reject/abstain; reuse the `DataCleaning Project` writeback.

## 10. Acceptance criteria
- **Yardstick exists:** off-diagonal confusion is measurable on a sealed slice nothing trained on (§3, §4).
- **Targeted boundaries improved:** `long→absurdly` fire-rate and the color confusion cells shrink on the sealed slice vs the prior checkpoint.
- **No broad regression:** the ~19,250 non-targeted tags' clean-slice mAP is not worse than the prior checkpoint.
- **Prior-corrected:** operating point matches the real-booru prior, not the gold prior (§6).
- **Domain honesty:** the synthetic:real training ratio is recorded and within an acceptable bound (§7).

---

## 11. Risks & non-negotiables
- **The fine-tune trap.** Do **not** fine-tune the biased V2 checkpoint on the small gold set; "low LR" does not make it safe (Kumar et al. 2022). Train on the full augmented corpus. (§5, §7)
- **Synthetic ≠ real.** The gold is Anima output — strong for generating clean *crisp* and *rare-positive* examples, weak on subtle ordinal boundaries; don't let it dominate the training mix. ([golden_set_collection_targets §8](../.research/golden_set_collection_targets.md#L189-L191))
- **Seal before you train.** Lock a disjoint clean eval slice first, or the yardstick is gone (V1's failure).
- **Cleaning is external.** V2.1.1 never decides a label is wrong — `Pipeline2_review` does. This is what keeps the diagonal blind spot out of the loop.
- **Prior-correct before trusting outputs.** Oversampled gold → analytic log-prior-ratio offset (§6).
- **Ordinal cure is deferred.** Cleaner data softens but does not structurally fix `long→absurdly`; the CORN/CORAL head is a later iteration.
